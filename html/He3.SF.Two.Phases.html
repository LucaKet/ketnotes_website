He3 superfluidity: the two phases

Once the unconv. SC theory of He3 is constructed (Symmetry of unconventional superconductivity (general)) one finds two solutions to the BCS equations [Note: make note], meaning one finds two $\gamma$ coefficient matrices that both solve the BCS equations.

These have been identified as two distinct phases of He3, one generally being more stable than the other (work by Leggett), but each having distinct features.
