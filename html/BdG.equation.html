BdG equation

Starting from the construction of the $4\times4$ BdG Hamiltonian one wishes to find its spectrum through the eigenvalue equation \[ H(k) \vec{v}(k) = E(k) \vec{v}(k), \tag{1} \] where: \[ \vec{v}(k) = (u_s(k), v_s(k)) \tag{2} \] is the eigenvector, where each entry is itself a vector of length $2$: $u_s(k) = [u_1(k), u_2(k)]^T_s$.

Note the (arbitrary) form of the dependencies on the momentum $k$: these are such because of the basis in which we are constructing the $4\times4$ hamiltonian starting from the original interacting theory: \[ [C^\dagger(k)_s, C(-k)_s], \tag{3} \] which, written with the original creation/annihilation operators are \[ C^\dagger(k)_s = [C^\dagger(k)_{\uparrow}, C^\dagger(k)_{\downarrow}] \tag{4} \] and so for $C(-k)_s$. (This is the case in which a Cooper pair is formed by one up and one down spin).
