Eigenstates of topological systems as monopoles

In systems with non trivial topologies, the eigenstates are usually not well defined in some point in their parameter space. A concrete example might be a $2$ state system whose eigenstates live on the sphere, that are parametrized by the angles $\theta$ and $\phi$. What one finds is that these states are not well defined — however one chooses the particular gauge — for some combination of these $2$ angles.

This has one important consequence: namely that the points where the eigenstates are not well defined become sources for the Berry potential → they imply a non zero Berry phase and Chern number: definition.
