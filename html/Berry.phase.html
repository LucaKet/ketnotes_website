Berry phase

As a hamiltonian undergoes a cyclic transformation throughout its parameter space its eigenfunctions acquire a physical phase. This phase is called Berry phase, and one can obtain a formula for it as: \[ \gamma_n = \int_{C} dR \ \langle n(R)|\nabla_R|n(R)\rangle \tag{1} \] where $n$ is simply the eigenstate ($n$ being the band label), $C$ the closed path in parameter space parametrized by $R$.

Source: Berry phase (Bernevig, Ch. 2)
