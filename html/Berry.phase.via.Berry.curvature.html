Berry phase via Berry curvature

To compute the Berry phase using the berry curvature, which is the curl of the Berry potential, we can make use of Stokes theorem, turning the line integral of the Berry potential into a surface integral \[ \gamma_n = \int_{S} dS \ \cdot \ F \tag{1} \] where $S$ is the surface enclosed by $C$ (of Berry phase) and $F$ is the Berry curvature as a magnetic field.
