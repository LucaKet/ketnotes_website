Dirac points

Dirac points are points in k-space where the dispersion is linear, meaning they can be modelled by \[ H = \hbar v_f (k_x \sigma_x \pm \ k_y \sigma_y) \tag{1} \] which has the structure of a cone.

The integral of the Berry potential around these cones is non-zero, meaning that their Berry phase is non-zero. In particular their Berry phase is $\pm \pi$.

source: A primer on Weyl Semimetals (Kar & Jayannavar, 2019)
