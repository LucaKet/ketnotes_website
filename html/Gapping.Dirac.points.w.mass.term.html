Gapping Dirac points with a mass term

The structure of a Dirac point in $2$ dimensions is given as \[ H = v_f (k_x \sigma_x \ \pm \ k_y \sigma_y), \tag{1} \] which leaves the freedom to add a term proportional to the missing (non trivial) Pauli matrix $\sigma_z$.

If this is added, \[ H = v_f (k_x \sigma_x + k_y \sigma_y) + \Delta \sigma_z, \tag{2} \] the spectrum of the Dirac points gets gapped out!

This is however not realized in real graphene as symmetries prevent a term proportional to $\sigma_z$ to appear. In particular time reversal and inversion symmetry do not allow for such a term to be non-zero.
