Berry potential

The Berry phase Berry phase can be re-written by simply defining the integrand as its own quantity, which is called Berry potential.

\[ A(R) \equiv \langle n(R) |\nabla_R| n(R) \rangle \tag{1} \]

so that the Berry phase is now

\[ \gamma_n = \int_{C} dR \ A(R) \tag{2} \]
