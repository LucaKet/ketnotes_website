Superfluid He3 and unconventional superconductivity (Annett, Ch. 7)

J. F. Annett, “Superconductivity, Superfluids, and Condensates”, Ch. 7.

pg150: The main idea behind Landau's theory is that wave functions of the full interacting systems can be found as adiabatic continuation of the ones of the non-interacting system. This of course does not take into account possible topological phase transitions, as a topological hamiltonian can not be achieved by adiabatic continuation of a non-topological one.

pg150: Luttinger theorem states that the position of the Fermi wave vector $K_f$ remains unchanged as the interaction is switched on in spherical systems.

pg152: The pairing interaction in He3 was recognized to be spin dependant as the magnetic susceptibility of He3 as computed in Landau theory is seen to be much higher once interactions are switched on. This suggested that the system tends to (ferro)magnetic order at low temperatures, thus implying that the pairing mechanism that gives rise to the SC phase might originate as a consequence of the polarization bubbles created by spin-spin interactions.
