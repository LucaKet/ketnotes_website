“Three prerequisites for high-temperature superconductivity in t-PtBi2” (2025).

pg2: the $3$ identified (general) prereqs for unconventional SC are:

  1. High DOS around the Fermi surface
  2. An interaction mechanism to form Cooper pairs [Q: are we sure cooper pairs are indispensable?]
  3. The ability to experimentally tune the material to enhance SC
