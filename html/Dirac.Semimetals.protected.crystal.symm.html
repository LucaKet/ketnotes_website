Dirac semimetals protected by crystal symmetry

A Dirac semimetal is described by a $4$ band hamiltonian that has a $4$-fold degenerate band crossing point at some k-space momentum. This is assured by the combination of Time reversal and Inversion symmetry!

However, such symmetries by themselves are easily broken by some mass-term perturbation.

Only an additional crystal symmetry can prevent such perturbation from appearing.
