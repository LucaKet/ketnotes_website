Quantum Field Theory (Wilczek, 1998)

F. Wilczek, “Quantum Field Theory” (1998).

pg1: The principles of QFT can be summarized as:

  • The constituents are operators (fields) which depend on space-time
  • The interactions between fields should be local → the equations (incl. comm. relations) should only depend on the space-time point and its derivatives.
  • symmetries must be preserved unless they are broken

pg2: The most fundamental fact of nature which QFT uniquely explains is the identical nature of the fundamental constituents: electrons and protons all look alike because they are the excitations of the same underlying field (one for the electron, one for the proton). W. concludes that thus the field is the underlying reality!
