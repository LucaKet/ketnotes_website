Altland–Zirnbauer classification of TI topological superconductors

For a Time Reversal invariant (TI) topological superconductor (SC) the Altland–Zirnbauer classification is based on the spin symmetries of the system:

  • DIII → no spin-rotation symmetry
  • AIII → $\sigma_3$ is conserved
  • CI → full spin rotation symmetry

thus any amount of spin orbit coupling places you in either class DIII or AIII.
